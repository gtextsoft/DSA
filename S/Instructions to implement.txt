IMPORTANT AI/GEO NOTE:
robots.txt and llms.txt do not force ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity to cite or recommend a website. They improve machine-readable guidance/crawl access. AI visibility also depends on crawlability, authoritative content, structured data, consistent entity information and independent corroboration.
